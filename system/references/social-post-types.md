## The 7 Intent Types

### 1. TEACH
**Purpose:** Build authority, attract followers, earn saves/shares.
**What it is:** How-to, quick tip, framework, myth-busting, step-by-step.
**X format:** Tip + image, or short thread (3-5 tweets). Specific numbers beat vague claims.
**LinkedIn format:** Long-form post (900-1500 chars) or carousel (suggest `/visual-carousel`).
**Facebook format:** Shorter caption + image or carousel repurpose.
**Example hook:** "Here's exactly how I replaced a $4K/month agency with one AI tool."

### 2. HOT TAKE
**Purpose:** Drive replies and debate. Show independent thinking.
**What it is:** Contrarian opinion, industry commentary, prediction, challenge conventional wisdom.
**X format:** Punchy 1-2 sentences. Designed to provoke replies (replies = 27x a like on X).
**LinkedIn format:** Opening statement + reasoning + "what do you think?"
**Facebook format:** Short take + question.
**Example hook:** "Everyone's building AI agents. Almost nobody is building AI judgment."

### 3. STORY
**Purpose:** Build connection, trust, and parasocial relationship.
**What it is:** Personal anecdote, behind-the-scenes, vulnerability, milestone, throwback.
**X format:** Short thread (3-5 tweets) or single tweet + photo. Photo of you/workspace preferred.
**LinkedIn format:** Narrative arc (setup, conflict, lesson). 900-1500 chars.
**Facebook format:** Shorter narrative + photo album or single image.
**Example hook:** "3 years ago I had a team of 45. Today it's just me and AI. Here's why."

### 4. ENGAGE
**Purpose:** Boost algorithm signals (replies/comments). Build community.
**What it is:** Question, poll, this-or-that, fill-in-the-blank, challenge.
**X format:** Direct question or poll. Keep it 1-2 sentences. Low friction = high participation.
**LinkedIn format:** Discussion question with brief context. Designed for thoughtful comments.
**Facebook format:** Question or poll. Casual tone.
**Example hook:** "What's the one marketing task you hate most? I'll show you how to automate it."
**Note:** ENGAGE posts are intentionally lightweight. They make the feed feel conversational.

### 5. PROOF
**Purpose:** Establish credibility without bragging. Show results.
**What it is:** Case study, screenshot + context, customer transformation, specific result.
**X format:** Screenshot + 1-2 sentence story. Or a single compelling stat.
**LinkedIn format:** Mini case study narrative. Focus on the transformation, not the flex.
**Facebook format:** Result screenshot + short caption.
**Example hook:** "Sent my first email in 6 months. 47% open rate. Here's what I did differently."
**Hard rule:** Never lead with dollar amounts or revenue. Frame around transformation, not wealth.

### 6. OBSERVE
**Purpose:** Show personality. Make the feed feel human and real.
**What it is:** Relatable moment, humor, cultural commentary, "is it just me?", random thought.
**X format:** Quick observation, 1-2 sentences. No lesson, no CTA. Just a thought.
**LinkedIn format:** Usually skip LinkedIn for OBSERVE (too casual). Use only if the observation has business relevance.
**Facebook format:** Short, shareable observation.
**Example hook:** "The gap between 'I should post on social media' and actually posting is approximately 6 months."
**Note:** OBSERVE posts are the most human type. They exist to break up the teaching/proving pattern. No agenda.

### 7. PROMOTE
**Purpose:** Convert followers to leads/customers. Soft sell.
**What it is:** Value-first CTA, offer mention, lead magnet, customer story as proof, waitlist.
**X format:** Teach something valuable, then CTA in reply (never in the main tweet, links kill reach).
**LinkedIn format:** Story or value post with natural CTA woven into the ending.
**Facebook format:** Value post + link in first comment.
**Example hook:** "I've been building marketing systems for small businesses using AI. If you want me to build yours, I'm taking 10 people this month."
**Frequency:** Max 1/week. Earn trust first, then ask. Ratio: 1 PROMOTE for every 13 value posts.

## Platform Algorithm Notes (2026)

**X/Twitter:**
- Replies weighted 27x > likes. Conversation chains weighted 150x > likes.
- Time decay: half visibility every 6 hours. First 30-60 minutes matter most.
- External links get near-zero distribution. Always put links in replies.
- X Premium gives 4x in-network visibility boost.
- Rich media (images, video, polls) outperforms plain text.

**LinkedIn (Brew 360):**
- Quality over quantity. 1-2 posts/week beats daily posting.
- Saves and meaningful comments are the strongest signals.
- Native video gets +69% performance boost (face in first 4 seconds).
- Expertise evaluated against your profile. Post within your niche.

**Facebook:**
- 50% of feeds now show content from accounts users don't follow. Discovery is back.
- Shares/saves are the strongest signals.
- Native video and Reels get preferential treatment.
- Personality-driven content from real people beats brand page content.

**Instagram:**
- Carousels get 2x engagement vs single images.
- Saves and shares are strongest signals.
- Reels still get preferential distribution for discovery.
- Face in first 2 seconds of Reels matters.
- No hashtags (content is surfaced semantically).
